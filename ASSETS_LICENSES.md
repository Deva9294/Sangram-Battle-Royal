# SANGRAM asset licences

## Animated soldier characters

Four animated GLB characters are downloaded by `tools/download_cc0_assets.sh` from Gobkit Free Character Pack Vol.4:

- Rifleman
- Recon Scout
- Army Sniper
- Combat Medic

Source: https://gobkit.com/freebies/human004/
Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication); commercial use and modification permitted without attribution.

The models are rigged and include Idle, Walk, Run, Death, Hit, Attack and Special animations at 30 FPS. The downloader is used in local setup and CI so the APK packages the assets for offline play.

Do not redistribute the raw asset pack as a standalone product. The assets are included only as part of the SANGRAM game.
